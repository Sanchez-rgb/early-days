Privacy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

The short version: Early Days stores your baby's records in your browser on your own device. There is no account, no cloud sync, and no analytics. We never receive your records.

What the app stores

When you use Early Days, the following stays in your browser's local storage on your device:

  • Your baby's nickname, birth date, and due date
  • Feeding, sleep, diaper, weight, length, and note entries you add
  • Milestones you mark as done
  • Your language preference and license status

This data is not transmitted to us or to any third party by the app. Clearing your browser data, or using the "Clear all" option in Settings, removes it.

What we do not collect

  • No account or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising SDKs
  • No cloud database of baby records
  • No cookies set by this website

Payments

Early Days Pro is sold through our payment partner, Waffo. When you buy Pro, Waffo processes the payment and we receive the email address and order identifier needed to issue your activation code. Waffo's own privacy policy applies to the payment information you provide there. We do not receive or store your card details.

Hosting

This website and the app are served as static files by GitHub Pages. Like most hosting providers, GitHub may keep standard access logs (such as IP address, browser type, and time of request) for security and operations. We do not have access to those logs as a marketing dataset. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Backups you create

If you export a backup, you choose where that file goes. It is your responsibility to store it safely. Importing a backup replaces the records in that browser, so keep a copy before importing.

Children's data

Early Days is intended for parents and caregivers. Information about a child is entered by the parent or caregiver and stays on their device. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new date. Material changes will be noted in the app or by email to Pro customers.
