About Early Days

Updated 22 November 2026

Early Days is an independent, local-first baby tracker for families who started early. It puts corrected age first, plots your baby's own curve, and keeps every record on your device.

Why corrected age comes first

Most baby apps count from the birthday. For a premature baby, that starting line is wrong. A baby born 12 weeks early is not 12 weeks behind; they are on a different calendar. Early Days asks for the due date once, shows corrected age on the home screen, and uses it for growth and milestones in the first two years.

The app was built around a simple observation from NICU parents: the number they need most is the one most apps bury in a settings screen.

What the app does — and does not do

  • Does: record feeding, sleep, diapers, weight, length, and notes; show corrected age; plot your baby's own weight curve; keep NICU and home milestones; export a backup; generate a printable album.
  • Does not: diagnose, assess development, recommend feeding changes, or compare your baby to percentile bands. It records and converts. Your pediatrician interprets.

Early Days is not a medical device. It does not upload records to a server. There is no account, no analytics, and no advertising SDK.

How the guides are written

The preemie guides are plain-language explanations for parents, not clinical guidance. Our approach:

  • Start from primary or authoritative sources — AAP policy statements, CDC milestone checklists, WHO growth standards, and the Fenton preterm growth charts.
  • Link the sources on each page so you can read them yourself.
  • Separate what is known from what is individual. Milestones are ranges; chart choice and cutoff ages are clinical decisions.
  • Show an updated date on every guide and correct errors when they are found.
  • Never present a general guide as a diagnosis or a treatment plan.

We are a small independent product team, not a clinical practice. If a guide conflicts with your pediatrician's advice, follow your pediatrician.

Privacy

Baby data is health data. Early Days keeps records in your browser's local storage on your device. You can export a backup whenever you want. Read the full privacy policy.
