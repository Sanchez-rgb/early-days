24 month corrected age milestones

Updated 23 November 2026

At 24 months corrected, many preemies are running, combining words, and showing more independence. This is also around the age when many pediatricians stop correcting for prematurity.

What 24 months corrected means

24 months corrected means the age your baby would be if they had been born on their due date. It is not the same as the age on the birth certificate.

Corrected age = actual age − days born early

A baby born 8 weeks early is about 25.8 months actual when they are 24 months corrected. The milestones below are listed at corrected age, because that is the timeline a premature baby's brain and body were developing on.

Milestones to look for

AreaExamples at 24 months corrected
Social / emotionalNotices when others are hurt; plays alongside other children; shows independence.
LanguageUses 50+ words and 2-word phrases; follows two-step directions.
MovementRuns; kicks a ball; walks up stairs.
ThinkingSorts shapes; uses a spoon; simple pretend play.

CDC describes milestones as things most children (about 75% or more) can do by a given age. They are examples, not a test, and not a diagnosis.

Play that supports this stage

  • Pretend play with simple props.
  • Puzzles with a few large pieces.
  • Drawing and coloring with chunky crayons.
  • Counting songs and naming games.

What not to worry about

  • Speech clarity varies; strangers may not understand everything yet.
  • Some children are late talkers and catch up.
  • Parallel play is normal; sharing comes later.

When to ask your pediatrician

  • No two-word phrases.
  • Does not follow simple directions.
  • Loses skills they previously had.

Questions

Is 24 months corrected the same as actual age?

No. Corrected age is measured from the due date. At 24 months corrected, actual age is larger by however many weeks early your baby was born.

Should I use corrected age for this checklist?

Yes. In the first two years, developmental milestones and screening are usually assessed at corrected age for premature babies.

What if my baby was born at 28 weeks?

At 28 weeks, your baby was 12 weeks early. At 24 months corrected, they are roughly 26.8 months actual.

Do we stop correcting at 2 years?

Many pediatricians switch to chronological age around 24 months, and some continue adjusted-age screening to 30 months. Ask your pediatrician what they recommend for your child.
