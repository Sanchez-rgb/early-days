2 month corrected age milestones

Updated 23 November 2026

At 2 months corrected, the milestones are about connection: calming to your voice, a first social smile, and brief head control. Here is what many preemies are doing, and what to do if you are unsure.

What 2 months corrected means

2 months corrected means the age your baby would be if they had been born on their due date. It is not the same as the age on the birth certificate.

Corrected age = actual age − days born early

A baby born 8 weeks early is about 3.8 months actual when they are 2 months corrected. The milestones below are listed at corrected age, because that is the timeline a premature baby's brain and body were developing on.

Milestones to look for

AreaExamples at 2 months corrected
Social / emotionalCalms when spoken to; smiles responsively; looks at your face.
LanguageMakes sounds besides crying; turns toward sounds.
MovementHolds head up briefly during tummy time; moves both arms and legs.
ThinkingWatches you as you move; looks at a toy for a few seconds.

CDC describes milestones as things most children (about 75% or more) can do by a given age. They are examples, not a test, and not a diagnosis.

Play that supports this stage

  • Short tummy-time sessions several times a day, awake and supervised.
  • Face-to-face talking, copying your baby's sounds back to them.
  • High-contrast cards or a mobile within their focus range.
  • Narrate care: “Now we change your diaper.”

What not to worry about

  • Head lag in the first weeks is common and improves with tummy time.
  • Startles and hiccups are normal at this age.
  • One late item is not a diagnosis; look at the pattern over weeks.

When to ask your pediatrician

  • No response to loud sounds.
  • Does not calm with comfort or feeding.
  • Stiffness or floppiness that worries you.

Questions

Is 2 months corrected the same as actual age?

No. Corrected age is measured from the due date. At 2 months corrected, actual age is larger by however many weeks early your baby was born.

Should I use corrected age for this checklist?

Yes. In the first two years, developmental milestones and screening are usually assessed at corrected age for premature babies.

What if my baby was born at 28 weeks?

At 28 weeks, your baby was 12 weeks early. At 2 months corrected, they are roughly 4.8 months actual.

Can I do tummy time if my baby was in the NICU?

Ask your baby's clinician. Many babies can start short, supervised sessions once they are stable, but the timing depends on their history.
