6 month corrected age milestones

Updated 23 November 2026

At 6 months corrected, the big shifts are rolling, supported sitting, and back-and-forth babbling. Many preemies are also reaching and bringing everything to their mouth.

What 6 months corrected means

6 months corrected means the age your baby would be if they had been born on their due date. It is not the same as the age on the birth certificate.

Corrected age = actual age − days born early

A baby born 8 weeks early is about 7.8 months actual when they are 6 months corrected. The milestones below are listed at corrected age, because that is the timeline a premature baby's brain and body were developing on.

Milestones to look for

AreaExamples at 6 months corrected
Social / emotionalKnows familiar people; laughs; looks at themselves in a mirror.
LanguageTakes turns making sounds with you; blows raspberries.
MovementRolls tummy-to-back; sits with support or leans on hands.
ThinkingReaches for toys; brings things to mouth; bangs objects.

CDC describes milestones as things most children (about 75% or more) can do by a given age. They are examples, not a test, and not a diagnosis.

Play that supports this stage

  • Peekaboo and “where did it go?” games.
  • Textured toys and safe objects to mouth.
  • Supported sitting with a pillow or your lap.
  • Naming familiar people and objects during the day.

What not to worry about

  • Sitting independently comes later for many preemies.
  • Crawling is not required; some babies bottom-shuffle or skip it.
  • Babbling sounds vary widely between babies.

When to ask your pediatrician

  • Does not roll in either direction.
  • No babbling or back-and-forth sounds.
  • Stiff legs, fisted hands, or a strong one-sided preference.

Questions

Is 6 months corrected the same as actual age?

No. Corrected age is measured from the due date. At 6 months corrected, actual age is larger by however many weeks early your baby was born.

Should I use corrected age for this checklist?

Yes. In the first two years, developmental milestones and screening are usually assessed at corrected age for premature babies.

What if my baby was born at 28 weeks?

At 28 weeks, your baby was 12 weeks early. At 6 months corrected, they are roughly 8.8 months actual.

Does my baby need to crawl?

No. Crawling is common but not a required milestone. Some babies bottom-shuffle, commando-crawl, or skip crawling and go straight to pulling up.
