9 month corrected age milestones

Updated 23 November 2026

At 9 months corrected, many preemies are sitting independently, getting mobile in their own way, and showing a clear preference for familiar people.

What 9 months corrected means

9 months corrected means the age your baby would be if they had been born on their due date. It is not the same as the age on the birth certificate.

Corrected age = actual age − days born early

A baby born 8 weeks early is about 10.8 months actual when they are 9 months corrected. The milestones below are listed at corrected age, because that is the timeline a premature baby's brain and body were developing on.

Milestones to look for

AreaExamples at 9 months corrected
Social / emotionalShows stranger or separation anxiety; responds to their name.
LanguageMakes “mamamama” and “babababa” sounds; understands “no” from tone.
MovementSits without support; crawls or commando-crawls; may pull to stand.
ThinkingRakes food toward themselves; bangs objects; looks for a dropped toy.

CDC describes milestones as things most children (about 75% or more) can do by a given age. They are examples, not a test, and not a diagnosis.

Play that supports this stage

  • Hide a toy under a cloth and let your baby find it.
  • Stacking cups and containers to fill and dump.
  • Safe finger foods during meals.
  • Name body parts and familiar objects.

What not to worry about

  • Crawling style varies; some babies never crawl.
  • Pulling to stand may come later for preemies.
  • Separation anxiety is a developmental step, not a regression.

When to ask your pediatrician

  • Does not sit without support.
  • No babbling or varied sounds.
  • Does not bear weight on legs when held upright.

Questions

Is 9 months corrected the same as actual age?

No. Corrected age is measured from the due date. At 9 months corrected, actual age is larger by however many weeks early your baby was born.

Should I use corrected age for this checklist?

Yes. In the first two years, developmental milestones and screening are usually assessed at corrected age for premature babies.

What if my baby was born at 28 weeks?

At 28 weeks, your baby was 12 weeks early. At 9 months corrected, they are roughly 11.8 months actual.

Is separation anxiety normal?

Yes. Around this age many babies become clingier with familiar caregivers and wary of strangers. It usually eases as mobility and language grow.
