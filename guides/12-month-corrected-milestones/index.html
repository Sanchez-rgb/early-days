12 month corrected age milestones

Updated 23 November 2026

At 12 months corrected, the milestones cluster around communication and movement: gestures, first words, pulling up, and maybe those first steps.

What 12 months corrected means

12 months corrected means the age your baby would be if they had been born on their due date. It is not the same as the age on the birth certificate.

Corrected age = actual age − days born early

A baby born 8 weeks early is about 13.8 months actual when they are 12 months corrected. The milestones below are listed at corrected age, because that is the timeline a premature baby's brain and body were developing on.

Milestones to look for

AreaExamples at 12 months corrected
Social / emotionalWaves bye-bye; plays pat-a-cake.
LanguageSays “mama” or “dada”; follows simple directions with a gesture.
MovementPulls to stand; cruises along furniture; may walk.
ThinkingUses a pincer grasp; finds hidden objects; stacks two blocks.

CDC describes milestones as things most children (about 75% or more) can do by a given age. They are examples, not a test, and not a diagnosis.

Play that supports this stage

  • Roll or gently bounce a ball back and forth.
  • Board books with flaps and familiar pictures.
  • Cause-and-effect toys: push, pop, open, close.
  • Safe finger foods and a cup to practice with.

What not to worry about

  • Walking at 12 months is common but not required.
  • Bow legs and flat feet are common in early walkers.
  • Word count varies; gestures count as communication.

When to ask your pediatrician

  • No words and no gestures (pointing, waving, reaching to be picked up).
  • Does not stand with support.
  • Loses a skill they previously had.

Questions

Is 12 months corrected the same as actual age?

No. Corrected age is measured from the due date. At 12 months corrected, actual age is larger by however many weeks early your baby was born.

Should I use corrected age for this checklist?

Yes. In the first two years, developmental milestones and screening are usually assessed at corrected age for premature babies.

What if my baby was born at 28 weeks?

At 28 weeks, your baby was 12 weeks early. At 12 months corrected, they are roughly 14.8 months actual.

Does my baby need to walk by 12 months?

No. Many preemies walk later, especially those born very early. Corrected age is the fair clock, and some children walk at 15–18 months corrected.
