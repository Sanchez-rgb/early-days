Questions

Straight answers.

What is corrected age?

Corrected age is your baby's actual age minus the number of days they were born before their due date. A baby born at 28 weeks (12 weeks early) who is 4 months old has a corrected age of about 1 month. It is the age pediatricians use for growth and milestones in the first two years.

How long should I use corrected age?

Many pediatricians use corrected age until about 24 months, and some extend it to 36 months for very premature babies. Follow your own pediatrician's guidance — Early Days only does the arithmetic.

Is Early Days a medical device?

No. It records and converts ages. It does not diagnose, assess development, or give medical advice. If something worries you, call your pediatrician.

Where is my baby's data stored?

In your browser's local storage on your own device. There is no account and no server receiving your records. You can export a backup file at any time.

Can my partner use the same records?

You can export a backup file and import it on another device. Automatic real-time sync between phones is not included yet — that is a deliberate trade-off for keeping the app account-free and offline-first.

Is there a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time $19.99 purchase. Logging, growth curves, and milestones are free forever.
